# Sorch AI — Product Vision

## Tagline
**"Screen faster. Place smarter."**

## The Problem
In maritime recruitment, speed is revenue. A Chief Engineer gets called by 3 agencies the same day — the first one wins. A 10-person human sourcing team maxes out at 200–350 meaningful conversations/day. When a 2,000-lead list drops, it creates a multi-day backlog. Best candidates go cold and accept competing offers.

## The Solution
Sorch AI is a vertical SaaS that replaces the manual top-of-funnel screening with an autonomous AI voice pipeline. The system:
1. Reads uploaded CVs or contact CSVs
2. Dials candidates automatically using local Indian numbers
3. Holds natural voice conversations in Hindi/Hinglish
4. Scores candidates 0–100
5. Writes results directly into the agency's live Google Sheet

## Target Customer
Maritime recruitment agencies in India (Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata) with 5–50 person teams currently paying human callers to do basic screening.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Scale:** Hit 2,000 leads the moment the list is uploaded
- **Speed:** Sub-800ms conversational latency
- **Language:** Native Hindi/Hinglish via Sarvam AI
- **Integration:** Direct Google Sheets write-back — no new tools to learn

## What We Are NOT
- Not a generic AI calling platform (we are maritime-specific)
- Not a CRM (we feed into existing workflows)
- Not a replacement for senior recruiters (we handle top-of-funnel only)

## Revenue Model
- SaaS subscription per seat/agency
- Usage-based pricing on call minutes
- Tiered plans: Starter (500 calls/mo), Growth (5,000 calls/mo), Enterprise (unlimited)
